A period-by-period tracker, built around your target

The Math

How attendance is calculated

The same formula powers your dashboard, analytics, and simulator.

Attendance % = (Present Periods ÷ Total Periods) × 100
Present = 36 periods
Total    = 40 periods

Attendance = (36 ÷ 40) × 100
= 90%
Present
Periods you attended.
Absent
Periods you missed.
Total
Present + Absent (cancelled periods aren't counted).
Target %
The minimum you want to stay above — 75% by default.
