Welcome

What Attendance Tracker does

Attendance Tracker is a lightweight web app built for students who need to know, at a glance, whether they're safely above their attendance target — not just what their attendance was last week, but what it needs to be next week.

Most colleges set a minimum attendance percentage students must maintain per subject. Falling below it can mean losing exam eligibility. Attendance Tracker keeps a running, period-by-period record of your presence in every subject, works out your percentage the same way your institution does, and tells you — in real numbers — how many classes you need to attend to get back on track, or how many you can safely miss and stay there.

The Math

How attendance is calculated

One formula, used consistently everywhere in the app — the dashboard, analytics, and simulator all agree because they all use this.

Attendance % = (Present Periods ÷ Total Periods) × 100
Present = 36 periods
Total    = 40 periods

Attendance = (36 ÷ 40) × 100
= 90%
Present
Periods you attended.
Absent
Periods you missed.
Cancelled
Periods that didn't happen — excluded from the total entirely.
Total
Present + Absent periods (cancelled periods aren't counted).
Target %
The minimum you've set yourself to stay at or above — 75% by default, editable in Profile.

Use It Anywhere

Install Attendance Tracker as an app

Attendance Tracker is a Progressive Web App — install it on your phone, tablet, or computer and it opens and feels like a native app, with an icon on your home screen and no browser bar.

Android / Chrome

  • Open the Attendance Tracker website.
  • Tap the install icon in the address bar, or open the browser menu.
  • Choose "Install app" or "Add to Home Screen".

Desktop

  • Open the site in a supported browser (Chrome, Edge).
  • Use the browser's Install option, usually in the address bar.
  • Launch Attendance Tracker like any other application.

Exact menu wording varies by browser and OS version. Once installed, the app keeps working offline for pages you've already visited, and syncs new data as soon as you're back online.
